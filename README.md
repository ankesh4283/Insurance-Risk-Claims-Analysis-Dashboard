# 🛡️ Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis  

### End-to-End Excel + Power BI Risk Intelligence Project  

An advanced insurance analytics project that transforms raw policy and claims data into measurable risk exposure insights and underwriting intelligence.

This project demonstrates:

- Insurance KPI engineering  
- Claim severity & frequency analysis  
- Demographic risk segmentation  
- Vehicle-based exposure modeling  
- Executive-level dashboard reporting  

---

## 📌 Business Context  

An insurance company required structured visibility into:

- Total claim exposure across policies  
- Claim frequency and severity trends  
- High-risk demographic segments  
- Vehicle-based claim concentration  
- Geographic risk distribution  

The objective was to build a reporting system that supports underwriting decisions, pricing optimization, and portfolio risk monitoring.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary  

Analyzed **37,542 active insurance policies** to evaluate claim behavior and total exposure.

### Key Portfolio Metrics

- 📄 **Total Policies:** 37,542  
- 💰 **Total Claim Amount:** $187.8M  
- 📉 **Average Claim Frequency:** 0.5 (1 claim per 2 policies)  
- 💵 **Average Claim Amount:** $5.0K  

### Risk Distribution Snapshot

- 🚗 Private vehicle claims account for **~80%+ of total claim exposure**  
- 🌍 Urban & Highly Urban zones contribute **~40%+ combined claim share**  
- 👥 Age group 26–55 represents **~55–60% of total claims**  
- 🎓 Bachelor’s & High School segments together contribute **50%+ of claims**  

### Core Insight  

Claim exposure is concentrated within specific demographic, vehicle, and geographic segments — enabling targeted underwriting instead of broad premium increases.

## 📈 Risk Intelligence & Insights  

### 🚗 Vehicle-Based Exposure  

- Private vehicles generate approximately **4x higher total claims** compared to commercial vehicles  
- Top 4 car brands contribute **~30%+ of total claim amount**  
- Claim severity increases in newer car models (post-2010), indicating higher repair costs  

Vehicle attributes significantly impact risk exposure.

---

### 👥 Demographic Risk Segmentation  

- Drivers aged 26–55 account for **over half of total claims**  
- Single & Married policyholders together represent **~70%+ of claim exposure**  
- Education level influences claim distribution patterns  

Demographics play a measurable role in claim concentration.

---

### 🌍 Geographic Concentration  

- Urban zones individually contribute around **20% of total claims**  
- Highly Urban areas show elevated claim frequency  
- Geographic clustering increases regional exposure risk  

---

### 👶 Household Risk Patterns  

- Policyholders with no additional listed drivers account for **~70%+ of total claim value**  
- Claim exposure decreases as additional drivers increase  

Household composition impacts portfolio risk dynamics.

---

## 💼 Strategic Recommendations  

- Adjust premium pricing for high-claim vehicle brands  
- Monitor urban-region exposure to prevent risk concentration  
- Apply demographic-adjusted underwriting scoring  
- Incorporate vehicle manufacturing year into severity modeling  
- Develop predictive risk scoring for high-frequency segments
